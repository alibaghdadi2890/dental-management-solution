# Who sees what: money, visits and discounts — October 2026

This update changes what each member of your team can see and do. The clinic owner now decides
who sees money and other dentists' work, and manages it from **Settings**.

The starting point is "deny by default": each role starts with only what it needs for its job.
You can switch anything back on, for a whole role or for one person.

## What changes for your team

### Dentists

A dentist can still treat patients, see each patient's full record (chart, diagnoses, treatment
plan, tooth history, images) and run and complete visits. What changes:

- **Visits:** a dentist sees only **their own visits**: the ones where they are the dentist, or
  that they started. Another dentist's visit no longer appears in the Visits list or on the Today
  board. The patient's chart still shows all the work done on each tooth.
- **Money:** a dentist no longer sees patient balances, payments, receipts, statements or the
  Payments screen. They still see the prices and the total of their own visit.
- **Payment at the end of a visit:** when a dentist completes a visit, the summary shows the
  visit's total and **"Handed to front desk for payment"**. The visit goes to the front desk's
  checkout queue as before.
- **Discounts:** a dentist can no longer give a discount or change a service's price. The
  discount field shows **"Ask the owner"**.
- **Refunds and balance adjustments:** these are now for the owner only.

### Front desk

- Front desk still sees every visit, takes payments, prints receipts and works the checkout queue
  on the Today board.
- Front desk can **no longer give discounts**, at checkout or anywhere else.
- Front desk no longer sees the **clinic's finances**: the Payments screen, the Unpaid tab of the
  Visits list, the Owes balance view and Balance column of the patients list, and the CSV
  exports. One patient's balance and account are still visible.

### Assistants

- Assistants still see every visit and help run them.
- Assistants no longer see patient balances and payments, or the prices of other dentists'
  visits in the Visits list.

### The owner

Nothing is taken away from the owner. The owner role always has every permission and cannot be
changed.

## How to switch things back on

Open **Settings** in the sidebar. It now has three tabs: **Dental chart**, **Staff** and
**Roles**.

**For a whole role**, go to **Settings › Roles** and pick the role, for example Front desk. Each
permission has a switch with a short explanation. Switch on what you need, then click **Save
changes** at the bottom. **Reset to default** brings a role back to how it started.

**For one person**, go to **Settings › Staff**, open the person and choose the **Access** tab.
Under **Additional permissions**, switch on what that person needs. Anything their role already
gives them is shown as "from {role}". Save to apply.

Changes apply on the person's next action. They don't need to sign out.

| To let someone…                                                       | Switch on                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| see every visit, not only their own                                   | See all clinic visits           |
| see a patient's balance, payments and receipts                        | See patient balances & payments |
| take payments                                                         | Record payments                 |
| see the Payments screen, unpaid visits, balances in lists and exports | See clinic finances             |
| give discounts or change a service's price                            | Apply discounts                 |
| refund or void a payment, or adjust a balance                         | Refund and adjust               |

Some permissions need another to make sense. When you switch on **Record payments**, **See
patient balances & payments** turns on with it.

## Rather not manage it yourself?

Ask us. We can set your roles and each person's access for you, from our side, and change them
whenever you like. You can still look at them in Settings at any time.

## Your own roles

You can now create roles of your own, for example "Clinic manager". In **Settings › Roles**,
click **New role**, give it a name, and start empty or from a copy of an existing role. Then
assign it to people in **Settings › Staff**.

A role is never deleted. Switch it off with its **Active** switch once nobody holds it. Every
change to a role or to a person's access is recorded in **Activity**.
